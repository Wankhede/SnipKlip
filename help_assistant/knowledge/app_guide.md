# SnipKlip App Guide

## Getting Started and Salon Onboarding
Route: /apps/salon-onboarding
After registering and signing in, complete salon onboarding before using operational features. Add the salon profile, primary branch address, working information, and required branch details. A new salon without a branch is automatically directed to onboarding.

## Dashboard and Business Overview
Route: /dashboard/default
The dashboard summarizes today's bookings, income, walk-ins, online appointments, and upcoming work for the selected branch. Complete onboarding and select a branch before relying on dashboard totals.

## Bookings and Appointments
Route: /apps/bookings/manage-bookings
Use Manage Bookings to review appointments. Choose Add Booking to select a customer, services, date, available slot, and assigned staff, then confirm the appointment. Use the edit or view action from the booking list to update an existing appointment.

## Customers
Route: /apps/customers/manage-customers
Use Manage Customers to search and review customer profiles. Choose Add Customer to create a profile using only information the customer has authorized the salon to store. Existing profiles can be viewed or edited from their row actions.

## Employees and Staff
Route: /apps/employees/manage-employees
Use Manage Employees to add staff, maintain their salon role and contact details, and review active team members. Access depends on the signed-in user's assigned role and subscription permissions.

## Services
Route: /apps/services/manage-service
Use Manage Services to define the services offered by a branch, including service name, pricing, duration, and availability. Keep service information current so booking slots and invoices are accurate.

## Inventory and Products
Route: /apps/e-commerce/product/manage-product
Use Manage Products to add and update retail or consumable products. Track product information and stock-related details from the inventory area. Confirm quantities before completing billing activity.

## Billing and Invoices
Route: /apps/invoices/manage-invoices
Use Manage Invoices to review bills. Choose Add Invoice to select the customer, services or products, quantities, discounts, and payment details. Verify the final amount before saving or sharing an invoice.

## Reports and Analytics
Route: /apps/reports/dashboard
Reports provide branch-level business insights based on recorded bookings, invoices, expenses, and staff activity. Select the correct branch and date range, and make sure daily operational data is complete before interpreting results.

## Memberships, Coupons, and Reviews
Route: /apps/membership/manage-membership
Memberships support repeat-customer plans, while coupons support controlled discounts. Reviews help monitor service feedback. Configure eligibility and validity carefully before making an offer available.

## Account, Branch, and Access
Route: /apps/profiles/account/basic
Account and salon profile pages maintain the signed-in user's permitted details. Branch, role, and subscription settings determine which navigation items and actions are available. Contact an authorized salon administrator when a required feature is not visible.

## Password and Account Safety
Route: /forgot-password
Use Forgot Password on the login page when you cannot sign in. Never share passwords, one-time codes, payment details, API keys, or customer information in the help chat. SnipKlip support will not ask for your password.

## Getting Help
Route: /contact-us
Ask this assistant general questions about where a feature is and how a workflow works. It cannot view salon records or perform actions. For account-specific, billing-provider, or unresolved technical issues, use Contact Us without including passwords, one-time codes, payment details, or unnecessary customer information.
